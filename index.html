About this site

Hi, I’m Nishant Chandel. I built this site to put the small calculators people look for every day in one clean, dark page that loads instantly, even on a slow connection.

There are no ads and no trackers. Signing in is optional and handled by Google Firebase. All the maths happens inside your browser.

  • Made for IndiaAmounts in ₹ with lakh and crore grouping, GST split into CGST and SGST, and time in IST.
  • PrivateNothing you enter in the tools leaves your device.
  • Works offlineOne file, no outside fonts or images. Only the optional sign-in loads Google’s Firebase library.
